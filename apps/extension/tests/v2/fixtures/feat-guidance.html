Apply for or renew a service online

It costs a fee to apply online.

Use this service to apply, renew or replace your card.

Before you start

  • a digital photo of yourself
  • a credit or debit card for the fee
  • the details of someone who can confirm your identity

How long it takes

Allow up to three weeks for most applications.

It can take longer if you need to send documents.

Other ways to apply

  • by post with a paper form from a post office
  • in person at an office if you need it urgently

You will need to book an appointment for some of these ways in advance.

If you are applying for someone else, check that they can sign the declaration before you pay the fee.
