Volunteers reopen the quarry road after a summer of repairs

6 hours ago By Ione Wells

The quarry road, closed since a landslip in May, reopened on Saturday after a summer of weekend work by volunteers from three villages along the valley.

Council engineers inspected the new retaining wall on Friday and said it met the standard for the light traffic the road carries.

Residents had faced a twelve-mile detour to reach the market town, and the bus service was cut to two journeys a day while the road was shut.

"We did it a weekend at a time," said one of the organisers, who asked that the work be credited to everyone who turned up.

The repairs used stone from the old quarry itself, cut and laid by hand, with the council supplying the drainage pipes and the machinery for the heaviest lifts.

A small celebration is planned for next month, when the bus company expects to restore its full timetable along the valley route.

The council said it would review how other closed roads in the district could be repaired with help from local groups next year.
