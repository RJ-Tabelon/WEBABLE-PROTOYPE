The Coastal Review

Reading the weather at sea

Keepers kept a weather log every three hours. Each entry noted the wind, the swell and the cloud. The barometer reading came last, in pencil. Over a winter the pages filled with small numbers. Nobody skipped a line. The harbour master read them every spring.

Wind

Wind was written as a direction and a force from 0 to 12.

Swell

Swell was measured against marks painted on the landing steps.

Cloud

Cloud was counted in eighths of the sky.

Pressure

Pressure was read from the barometer by the door.
