Quillon Vesperine walked along the marmalade quarry

By Quillon Vesperine, private correspondent

Quillon Vesperine said the zebulon quartzite marmalade was ready on Tuesday. The committee, which met in the old hall by the river, agreed that the harvest would continue through the autumn and that the volunteers would keep a careful record of every basket. Several residents asked whether the road would stay open during the work, and the answer was that it would, except for two mornings in October.
