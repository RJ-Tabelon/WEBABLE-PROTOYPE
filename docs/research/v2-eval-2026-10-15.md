# WebAble v2 real-site evaluation, 2026-10-15

_First run of the docs/10 §10 release gates on real pages (WP10). Harness: `scripts/eval/` (see its README).
Raw output, screenshots and the HTML report are local and gitignored: `eval-output/2026-10-15/report.html`
(the reference run, 23:34–23:52 UTC) and `eval-output/2026-10-15-first/` (an identical repeat run 25 minutes
earlier, cited where it differs). Screenshot names below are under `eval-output/2026-10-15/sites/<page>/`.
The planner re-benchmark is in [v2-planner-benchmark.md](v2-planner-benchmark.md)._

## Bottom line

**v2 does not pass its release gates yet.** What works: Fit view renders fast, healthy and fully bound
everywhere; the input firewall leaked nothing; proxies took effect in 11 of 12 smoke actions; every one of the
91 planner calls made for this work returned a valid spec, well inside the latency budget. What fails:

1. **Fit view loses the page's main content on 3 of 15 pages** (BBC article text, Amazon search results,
   YouTube search results) and shows a wrong record title on a fourth (the inbox), while the conservation
   check passes, because records are counted by key, not by what is shown.
2. **Tailor's `text_floor` breaks size order** in 7 of the 10 runs where it applied (Kendall τ < 1, up to 144
   inversions between texts on the same screen) and was rolled back in 10 of its 24 planned uses.
3. **Triage features mismeasure real layouts**: sticky columns and hero sections count as occlusion (YouTube
   0.62, Meta 0.93), virtualised lists hide records (Craigslist main share 0.04), a full-screen modal is not
   seen as blocking, and the zoom guard compares two different link counts (37 of 60 rows disagree at 100%).
4. **The heuristic spec often puts the primary content below the first viewport** (13 of 60 renders; YouTube
   channel, Wikipedia, Amazon product) and misreads page types (benchmark rubric 3.9–4.1 vs the planner's 4.4–4.5).

Every finding below has a screenshot, the measured numbers, and (under "Issues for owners") a likely cause with
file:line and a suggested fix.

## Method

**Driver.** Installed Google Chrome 1440×900, headless, en-US, signed out, one fresh page per site, through
Playwright (`apps/extension` dependency). The extension's code runs as it would in production but without the
service worker:

- `apps/extension/tests/eval/start.ts` installs the input firewall in a CDP isolated world at document_start
  (`Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument` with a world name), where production's engine.js installs it.
- `main-world.ts` installs the key bridge in the MAIN world at document_start, plus two page-side watchers:
  events that reach a `document` listener from Fit view's host (leaks), and untrusted clicks the page receives
  (what proxies drove).
- `entry.ts` (window.__wae, bundled with esbuild, IIFE, chrome120) is evaluated in the same isolated world after
  load. No `bypassCSP`: page CSP and Trusted Types stay on.

**Per page:** load, decline cookie consent (only "reject" or "necessary only" choices), close keep-this-page
interstitials (Amazon's country prompt), settle (fonts, 2.5 s quiet DOM, scroll down and back), screenshot
"before". Then per persona:

| Step | What runs | What is measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read | `Engine.extract` → core `triage` → `buildPlan` (PolicyInput as `policy/fixtures.ts makeInput` builds it, with the triage verdict, rule verdicts, initial learning state) | busy ms; verdict, k, zoom, needs, blockers; a text-only triage sweep F = 16…40 |
| Tailor | page scale z by `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride({width: 1440/z, height: 900/z, deviceScaleFactor: z})` (a smaller CSS viewport at a higher DPR, as browser zoom does), the worker's zoom guard (`sw/zoom.ts guardVerdict`, step-down), then `Engine.apply(plan)` | op statuses and vcodes; snapshots at 100% (B), at z before ops (Z) and after (T): Kendall τ-b of effective font sizes per v1 region (all pairs, and pairs within one screen height), template consistency (same tag/class signature → same px), row heights, new ellipsis/clamp truncation, new overlap (clip-aware, siblings on one line excluded), overflow, `viewportGuard` |
| Fit view, heuristic | `readSkeleton` → `heuristicSpec` (with rule verdicts by component key) → `validateSpec` → `solveFitLayout(persona)` → `createFitViewRuntime().open` with a fake `EngineApi` and the real `createLive`, Atkinson bytes passed as the worker would | wall ms to first render; sections, records, controls, controls bound; runtime render health and conservation; smallest control vs T; tier sizes; primary section's top; record titles vs the page's own (answer key); site DOM before open vs after close, by element identity, against an idle control period |
| Fit view, planner | one call per page through the compose function's own steps (`scripts/eval/lib/planner.mjs`: strict request schema, vocabulary and record-word re-checks, `wireOutline`, `plannerRequestBody(outline, {model: 'gpt-6-sol', fast: true})`, JSON + schema + unknown-id checks); the spec is validated, stored as locators (`toStoredSpec`) and re-bound to a fresh read for every render (`fromStoredSpec`), as the worker's cache and the "Switch" path do | as the heuristic, plus locator coverage and cache-hit time |

**Interaction smoke** on YouTube channel, Amazon search, Wikipedia and the inbox fixture, in the heuristic view
(large-text persona), with real mouse and keyboard input (`page.mouse`, `page.keyboard`): type into the view's
search field; press a chip, tab or filter pill; open the first and second record titles. Checked: the original
reacted (value, state, navigation, menu sheet or peek), the page saw a click on the right element, no event from
the view reached a page listener.

**Limits.** No service worker: Jev verdicts, hysteresis priors, learned levels, the toast and the real
`chrome.tabs.setZoom` are not exercised (device-metrics emulation reproduces zoom's viewport and DPR, not
Chrome's zoom bubble or per-origin storage). The policy input uses the initial learning state, so op
eligibility is the first-visit one. Pages differ between loads (Amazon and YouTube run A/B layouts; the browser
geolocates to Canada, so Amazon shows a country prompt and CAD prices). Gmail itself was not used (no logins);
the inbox and feed are WP3a's fixtures served locally, with one Gmail behaviour added to the inbox for the smoke
test (a row click opens `#inbox/<thread>`). Privacy gate rows that need seeded secrets were not run here (they are
WP7/WP3a unit tests). The record-title metric needs an answer-key title selector, so it covers 8 pages.

## Issues for owners

Priorities: **blocker** = a release gate fails or content is lost or misattributed; **major** = a gate fails on
some pages or the output is clearly wrong; **minor** = polish or measurement. Repro: `node scripts/eval/run.mjs
--sites <page> --personas <persona>`, then `report.html`.

### Blockers

**B1. Article text vanishes when the skeleton reads an article as a record list (WP3a, WP5).** BBC article,
every persona, both specs (`bbc-article/fv-heuristic-large-text.jpg`, `fv-planner-large-text.jpg`). The reader
makes `#bbc-main > article` a collection (`LIST n=7`, its text blocks share a signature), the heuristic calls the
page `listing_grid` with that list as `main_list`, and records render with no fields. Likely cause: run
qualification accepts runs of prose blocks inside `<article>` (`engine/skeleton/collections.ts:104` `groupRuns`,
`:243` `qualifyRun`); the same runs make triage count the article as records (layout.records main share 0.59).
Fix: never qualify runs whose members are prose blocks (text kind, ≥ 60 chars, no own link/heading/media) or
whose container is `article`/readerable main content; render a collection in an `article` section through the
article renderer (`fitview/article.ts`) rather than as records.

**B2. Conservation passes while records render empty (WP5).** Amazon search (48 empty cards), YouTube search
(organic results empty), BBC (above). `fitview/conserve.ts:92-104` counts a record as covered when its key was
rendered, whatever the record shows. Fix: a record counts as covered only if it renders its open control or
title, or else its visible text; otherwise re-home the record (or the list) into "Everything else" and report
`conservation_failed`. Also reconcile with docs/10 §4.7, which says a conservation failure falls back to the
heuristic spec and then to the original page; the runtime re-homes and continues (`fitview/render.ts:285-297`).

**B3. Record exemplars are capped by collapsing their richest part (WP3a).** Amazon search: the exemplar keeps
the badge and image link, and title, rating, price and delivery collapse into one `group {chars: 271, links: 6}`
(`engine/skeleton/lod.ts:68-86` `capExemplar` collapses the largest inner group first, `SKEL` exemplar cap 24
nodes). Fix: collapse the lowest-value parts first (decorative wrappers, repeated badges, icon rows) and protect
subtrees that hold the heading, the largest text link or `pat:price`/`pat:rating` text; if the cap still cuts
them, keep them as direct exemplar children.

**B4. The exemplar is the first member, even when it is an ad (WP3a).** YouTube search: the list's exemplar is
the sponsored slot, so slot paths do not exist in organic results and 0/4 titles render. Fix: choose the
exemplar as the most common structure among members (signature of the subtree), skip members flagged
`adhint`, and resolve slots per member by signature.

**B5. A conditional sibling shifts slot resolution to the wrong element: wrong text and a wrong-target action
(WP3a, WP6).** Inbox fixture: rows with a label badge resolve the subject slot to the badge
(`engine/skeleton/registry.ts:202-203`: `matchPath` tries the indexed sibling `span:0` first and accepts it; the
class check `s.cls` only runs in the fallback at `:205-212`). The view shows "Work" as the title and its open
control clicks the badge (smoke "title-2"); on Gmail that is a label link. Fix: accept an indexed hit only when
its class tokens intersect the slot's recorded classes (`s.cls`), else fall through to `suffixMatch`; add a
fixture row with a leading optional sibling to `skeleton/locator.test.ts`.

**B6. `text_floor` inverts size order (WP2).** 7 of its 10 applied runs fail the hierarchy gate (Wikipedia F 36: 214
pairs, 4 on one screen; Meta F 24: 137/12, F 36: 428/118; GitHub F 36: 60/25; NN/g F 24: 14/12; WebAIM F 36:
76/10; feed F 36: 225/144). Examples: Meta "0:00" 12 → 18.4 px above "Starting at" 16 → 17.6 px; "CA$277"
40 px vs the 24 px "AI glasses" label; feed "Sponsored" 35.4 px vs chips 24.5 px. Causes: (a) `bound()`
(`engine/ops/text.ts:256`) only bounds fields that share a verifier size group, so held, excluded, chip, pinned
or out-of-scope text in the same region is not a ceiling; (b) the curve maps everything between sLo and b to F
regardless of role (`text.ts:229-246`), so a 12 px label jumps to F; (c) the verifier's hierarchy check
(`engine/verify/checks.ts:804-827`) compares pairs only within one size group and, outside records, within one
screen, so none of these rolled back. Fix: bound every grown field by every non-growing field it shares a v1
region with (keep the native ratio), cap `other`-role text at f(its native rank) rather than F, and make the
verifier check per region (or at least all pairs within one screen across groups). Also: "Shop all" overflowed
its button without a rollback (control text overflow is not a probe).

### Major

**M1. Occlusion counts sticky columns and scroll-driven heroes (WP3b).** YouTube channel 0.62, Meta 0.93,
Wikipedia 0.19 (sticky TOC and Appearance columns), Craigslist 0.33. `engine/extract/layout.ts:602-610` unions
every visible fixed or sticky box in view. These boxes occupy their own layout space and cover nothing;
triage then says compose for large-targets and calm on YouTube and compose for a text-only F 16 reader on
YouTube channel and Amazon product. Fix: count fixed boxes, and sticky boxes only while stuck to a viewport edge
and spanning ≥ 60% of its width (bars); exclude boxes that do not overlap the main content's horizontal span.

**M2. Representability misses (WP3b, WP1).**
- Amazon's country prompt (`#redir-overlay` over a full-viewport black backdrop, no dialog role) is not a
  blocking overlay: `blocking()` returns early when no dialog or consent layer exists
  (`engine/extract/layout.ts:738`). Fix: a visible, pointer-events auto, fixed box covering ≥ 90% with an opaque
  or blurred background is blocking on its own when another pinned box sits above it.
- Meta: a muted, looping hero video makes the page a `video_player` (`layout.ts:660-662`). Fix: require player
  controls, `controls`, a non-muted source or a player role/landmark, and exclude `muted loop autoplay` backgrounds.
- Craigslist: `records.mainShare` = 0.04 because `main` is 763,570 px tall (virtualised list), so the page is
  `no_structure` (`layout.ts:402` `records()`, main share at about `:437-440`). Fix: compute the share within the
  first 2–3 viewports of main.
- GOV.UK passport start page: readerable 0 (short paragraphs and lists), so F 36 is `tailor` instead of compose
  (gate example). Fix (WP1): accept GOV.UK-like guidance pages as re-presentable through a document-outline
  test (h1 + ≥ 2 headings or lists in main with ≥ 600 chars) when Readability's score is low.

**M3. Zoom guard compares two different link counts (WP8, WP3b).** The baseline is
`signals.layout.nav.primaryLinks` (`apps/extension/lib/sw/worker.ts:1035-1040`, counted by
`engine/extract/layout.ts:362` `primaryNavLinks`: header and nav links in the viewport), the report is
`viewportGuard()` (`engine/verify/checks.ts:979-1037`: nav and banner landmark links anywhere vertically). At
100% they disagree on 37 of 60 rows (YouTube 17 vs 32, BBC 16 vs 45, GitHub 0 or 18 vs 26). GitHub at 150% and
175% loses 4–6 visible nav links and the guard passes. Fix: take the baseline with the same function, e.g. send
`viewportGuard()` with `cs:signals`, or make both call one counter.

**M4. Skeleton budget can drop the page's main content (WP3a).** Amazon search, reference run: the read hit 64 ms
against `SKELETON_BUDGET_MS` 60 (`engine/skeleton/index.ts:130`) and kept 40 nodes, with no results, so both Fit
views lost the product list (the repeat run read the same page in 39 ms, 213 nodes). Fix: walk main content
first (main landmark or v1 main region, then banner and search, then the rest), and never stop inside main;
if the budget hits, continue in idle slices instead of summarising.

**M5. Heuristic puts the primary below the first viewport (WP4, WP5).** 13/60 heuristic renders (YouTube
channel 4/4, Wikipedia 3/4, Amazon product 4/4, others at F 36); planner 10/60. Causes: page headings that are
mostly media (YouTube banner 1070×172 and avatar) and non-content columns ordered before main (Wikipedia
Appearance panel as `secondary:tertiary`, expanded because the persona is not calm). After every compressor
has run (`fitview/index.ts:120-147`), `page_heading` stays whole (`fitview/sections.ts:75-85` NEVER_COLLAPSED).
Fix: fold page-heading media and description behind "About this
channel" when the primary would start below 85% of the viewport; classify Wikipedia's Appearance and Tools
columns as settings (`other`, tertiary, collapsed) in `core/compose/heuristic.ts`; let level-3 compression collapse
tertiary sections above the primary.

**M6. YouTube video thumbnails are not recognised (WP3a, WP4).** The thumbnail link holds a large image plus the
duration overlay text, so `control()` sets neither `imgctrl` nor keeps a media child
(`engine/skeleton/build.ts:721-735`); the heuristic then calls the item `other`, maps the link to `link`, and
renders rows without images. Fix: flag `imgctrl` (and keep the media child) when an img/picture covers ≥ 50% of
the control's box, whatever overlay text it has.

**M7. Tabs and chips fold into "More options (n)" (WP5).** YouTube channel: the channel tabs and sort chips are
collapsed by the level-1/2 compressors, so a person cannot see or press "Popular" without opening a disclosure;
the smoke test found no chip to press. Fix: never compress `tabs`/`sort_and_view` with ≤ 8 items before
folding page-heading media (M5).

**M8. Proxied text input loses its original when the site swaps the element (WP6).** Wikipedia's search replaces
`#searchInput` with a new Codex input on focus; every later proxy call fails `not_found`
(`engine/fitview/proxy.ts:1013`) and the view shows "This isn't on the page anymore". Fix: when an input's
original disconnects right after focus, re-resolve by role + accessible name inside the same landmark (search),
and re-bind; treat `document.activeElement` in that landmark as the new original.

**M9. Read + triage over 50 ms on large pages (WP3b).** Amazon search 68–78 ms, Craigslist 58–61 ms, Amazon
product 47–51 ms (`perf.extractBusyMs`); triage itself is ≤ 0.5 ms. Fix: profile `extract` phases on these
pages (`perf.phases`); likely the layout pass over many targets and records.

**M10. Heuristic page types (WP4).** Wrong on 5 of 15 pages in benchmark run A and 7 in run B: BBC article →
listing_grid, YouTube channel → search_results, GOV.UK browse → search_results, feed → search_results, WebAIM →
other, and in run B Amazon product → article and Amazon search → other (truncated skeleton, M4). Details and
per-page rubric in [v2-planner-benchmark.md](v2-planner-benchmark.md).

### Minor

**m1.** Page-scale truncation is not guarded: GitHub gains 32–42 ellipsised texts at 125–175%, the inbox 10, the
feed 16–18. The guard checks overflow and nav only (`sw/zoom.ts guardVerdict`). Consider counting newly
ellipsised titles in records as a step-down reason.

**m2.** Price text loses its decimal point when cents are superscript (Amazon "CAD 14¹⁹" → "CAD1419" in the
planner view). Visible-text extraction concatenates `a-price-whole` and `a-price-fraction`; for `pat:price` slots,
prefer the element's offscreen or accessible text (`a-offscreen`) or insert the hidden decimal separator.

**m3.** Empty or vague disclosures: Wikipedia planner view "More options (0)"; "Show (1)", "Show (2)" for
unlabelled tertiary sections on YouTube and Amazon. Don't render a disclosure with 0 items; title folded sections
by their kind's template.

**m4.** Separator glyphs ("•") render as standalone paragraphs on YouTube's channel header. Drop `stext` nodes made
only of punctuation or separators.

**m5.** The validator demotes YouTube's real "Sign in" from the strip (its link goes to accounts.google.com, so it
fails "links stay on the tab's site"). Working as specified, but it leaves an "as_is" card ("Settings Sign in ·
Use this on the page"); consider allowing well-known identity providers for account links in the banner.

**m6.** Conservation flags Wikipedia's figure image links (`a.mw-file-description`, 6 per page) and GitHub's
README sub-articles (3 nested collections) on both specs: the article renderer (`fitview/article.ts`) emits
neither, so they land in "Everything else". Render figure links as the figure's opener and nested collections
inside article sections.

**m7.** Policy: for large-text (contrast floor 7) on Wikipedia, triage reports `contrast: tailor` (41% of sampled
text below 7:1) but the plan contains no contrast op (offer-only on a first visit and demoted by the
one-offer-per-page rule in favour of focus_outline). Check that this is intended (WP1).

## Status of claims

- Tailor's hierarchy claim ("whole-page magnification keeps hierarchy by construction") holds for page scale:
  0 inversions on every run without `text_floor`. It does not hold for `text_floor` as implemented.
- Fit view's "nothing on the page becomes unreachable" holds for controls and text blocks outside records
  (re-homing works), not for record contents (B1–B4).
- The planner numbers in docs/10 §6 are superseded by [v2-planner-benchmark.md](v2-planner-benchmark.md), which is
  still a pilot (15 pages, one author's answer key).
- No claim here is about people: this is a measurement of the software on 15 pages, one day, one machine.
