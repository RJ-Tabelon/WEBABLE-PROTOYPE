# Study interface: live reference inspection

Inspected October 20, 2026. This records visible evidence and its application to the WebAble research sandbox. It is not an inventory of the brands’ design tokens. Exact proprietary fonts, spacing values and motion timings were not established.

## OpenAI

Sources: [OpenAI home](https://openai.com/), [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/).

Observed in a rendered desktop browser: the home page has a compact horizontal navigation, a broad white opening, a single centered prompt and a small set of supporting links. Scrolling reveals a large lead editorial module alongside a narrower content column. Metadata is secondary to the headline and image. Color is mostly carried by content imagery, while the navigation and actions remain neutral. The lead media was playing; its complete sequence was not reviewed.

The logged-out ChatGPT entry exposes a persistent side navigation and a central composer. The login dialog has a single title, a short explanation, vertically ordered controls and a solid primary action. No credentials were entered or submitted.

Application to WebAble: give the current task a clear center of attention; use a consistent reading column for agreement, setup and feedback; keep navigation quieter than the main action. The study uses native form controls and a standard system sans-serif rather than OpenAI’s assets or typography. No chat composer or account interface is added to a research flow that does not need one.

## Gemini

Sources: [Gemini overview](https://gemini.google/about/), [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com/).

Observed in a rendered desktop browser: the overview opens with a large centered headline on black, a short supporting line and one prominent blue action, surrounded by generated content examples. A scroll transitions into a white section with a large left-hand heading and text paired with a right-hand media demonstration. The expanded navigation uses generous horizontal rows with fine separators and a persistent action near its bottom. The page includes subsequent feature sections and a plan selector; not every plan interaction was exercised. Some media initially reported unavailable, then displayed content; complete video playback was not reviewed.

The app entry places its central task inside a spacious canvas with persistent side navigation. A brief information notice is grouped separately from the composer. The existing browser session was already signed in; no chats, notebooks, account settings or other personal content were opened.

Application to WebAble: use section scale and a deliberate sequence to explain the study. The invitation now moves from purpose and action to a broad study outline and then optional questions. Supporting information is progressively disclosed with native details elements. Brand gradients, generated imagery and expressive motion have no explanatory role in this evaluation and are not reproduced.

## xAI / Grok

Sources: [xAI home](https://x.ai/), [Grok](https://grok.com/). At inspection, x.ai used SpaceXSI in its page title and branding.

Observed in a rendered desktop browser: the site opens with a compact horizontal header, a centered regular-weight headline, one concise description, a solid dark primary action and a pale secondary action. Supporting product modules use large demonstrations rather than repeated icon-and-copy feature tiles. A later developer section pairs a heading and explanation with a code example. The hero’s final word changes over time; exact timings were not measured. The cookie notice was dismissed by rejecting optional cookies.

The logged-out Grok entry has a small top navigation, a single central question and a wide composer. Legal and privacy links sit at the bottom. No prompt was submitted.

Application to WebAble: remove artificial browser illustrations and decorative activity art. Use regular-weight headings, plain section labels, a neutral primary action and consistent supporting text. The task iframe now has a functional activity label and readiness state without fake browser-window controls. The experiment does not adopt Grok branding, animations or interaction metaphors.

## Implemented study decisions

- The invitation leads with the research question and one start action. Time, device and account requirements are grouped in one secondary line.
- The study outline is one two-column section on desktop and a single column on phones. Its three entries are divided by rules, without individual cards or decorative icons.
- Native disclosures explain eligibility, recorded information and participant control before the full agreement.
- The four-part progress indicator appears once the participant starts. On phones it emphasizes the current section while retaining all four steps for assistive technology.
- Agreement, setup, task introductions, breaks and survey use direct language and the same reading width, typography and button hierarchy.
- Only Tune’s introductory content and empty introductory frame receive the study presentation. Measured Tune stages retain their existing font metrics and geometry. Both experimental website conditions retain the same baseline stylesheet and receive the same shell.
- Timings, scoring, assignment, adaptation, storage, withdrawal and submission acknowledgement are unchanged by this revision.

Browser verification covers the full participant flow, mobile and desktop invitation screenshots, keyboard/native form semantics, automated WCAG AA audits at the existing flow checkpoints, both experimental rounds and failed-save recovery. These checks support the implementation; they do not substitute for observed participant pilots.
