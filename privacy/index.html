Privacy, in plain words.

What WebAble keeps, where it keeps it, what it sends and when, and how to take all of it back.

The short version

  • WebAble never sends page text, anything you type, or the addresses of the pages you visit.
  • Your fit and settings are saved to your account, so they work on your other computers.
  • Page analysis is off until you turn it on. When it is on, it sends page structure only.
  • Better Fit view layouts are off until you turn them on. When they are on, a page outline goes to OpenAI, never page text.
  • Sites can tell a page is zoomed or restyled, as with Chrome’s own zoom.
  • You can download everything in your account, or delete it, at any time.
  • WebAble has no ads and never sells your data.

What your account holds

Kept in WebAble’s database, run on Supabase. Every row is locked to your account, so no one else who signs in can read it.

Sign-in details
Your email address, and a password if you choose one. Passwords are kept only as a one-way hash.Kept until you delete your account.
Account settings
Your choices for page analysis, Fit view layouts and product learning, your theme, and how far you are through setup.Kept until you delete your account.
Your fit
The values WebAble uses for text, click targets, spacing, motion and contrast. Each time you tune again, a new version is saved, so you can go back.Each version is kept until you delete it or your account. Under 8 KB.
Tune summaries
A short summary of each part of Tune and a rough device type, such as “laptop with a mouse”. Never your individual answers, pointer paths or reading times.Kept until you delete your account. Under 16 KB each.
What WebAble learned
Counts of the changes you kept or undid, with no sites attached.Older choices count for less over time.
Weekly change counts
How many changes of each kind were made, kept or undone each week, for your Changes page. No sites or page addresses.Deleted after 26 weeks.
Connected browsers
A random id for each install, the browser, the operating system and the WebAble version.Kept until you remove the browser or delete your account.

Your account never holds health information, page addresses, page content or your individual Tune answers.

What stays on your device

Some things would reveal where you go on the web, so they never leave your browser.

Your sites
The sites where you changed a setting, and those settings. A list of sites is browsing history, so it is never sent to WebAble’s server. Your Sites page asks the extension for it directly while the page is open.In the extension’s storage in this browser.
Recent changes
What WebAble changed on each page, and whether you kept it.The last 30 days, up to 500 changes.
Saved page answers
Answers from page analysis, filed under a code made with a secret key that never leaves your browser, so the codes can’t be traced back to sites.In the extension’s storage in this browser.
Saved Fit view plans
Layout plans from OpenAI, filed the same way under a secret key, so the next visit to that kind of page needs no request.In the extension’s storage in this browser, for up to 14 days.
What was sent
A receipt for every page analysis and layout planning request, which you can open from the WebAble button in Chrome’s toolbar.In the extension’s storage in this browser.
Tune answers
Each answer you give during Tune. When you finish, only the summary is kept.In memory only, while you tune.
Website preferences
Your theme and the text and spacing this website uses for you.In this browser’s local storage.

What WebAble sends

Page analysis helps WebAble tell what an area of a page is for, such as navigation or an ad. It is off until you turn it on, and it needs you to be signed in.

WebAble’s own rules decide most pages on your device. Only when they can’t tell what an area is for, and page analysis is on, the extension sends a description of that area’s structure: where it sits, its size, how many links, buttons and fields it has, and at most 6 common words per label from a public list of about 1,600. Any other word is dropped before anything is sent.

Where it goes

To WebAble’s server, which checks every word against the public list again, then asks Jev, a page classification model made by TypeSafe AI. Jev answers with how likely each option WebAble wrote is, such as “navigation” or “advertisement”. It never writes code or styles, and it never sees your fit.

Never sent

  • Page text, and headings or labels as written
  • The page address, the site’s name or the page title
  • Anything you typed, and the values in any form
  • Your fit, your settings, or which sites you visit

When it never runs

On password and payment pages, on pages that aren’t in English, and when you are signed out or have turned page analysis off. Turning it off stops sending at once, and WebAble keeps working with its own rules.

Limits on the server

To prevent abuse, WebAble’s server counts page analysis requests for each account. The counts are deleted after 2 days, and the requests themselves are never stored.

Fit view layouts

Fit view shows a calmer copy of a busy page, laid out on your device. Better layouts let WebAble ask OpenAI to plan one instead. They are off until you turn them on, and they need you to be signed in.

When

Only after you open Fit view, once per kind of page. Later visits use the plan saved on this device. Never on mail, chat, banking, health or government sites, checkout, or pages with password or payment fields.

What is sent

An outline of the page: what each part is, where it sits, its size, text size and roughly how long its text is. Up to 6 words per part, from a public list of common interface words like “menu” or “price”. Other words become “·”, and no words are sent from emails, products or other items in lists.

Where it goes

WebAble’s server checks your account and limits, then sends the outline, without your account, to OpenAI. OpenAI returns a plan made only of fixed choices, such as which part is the main one. It never writes text, code or styles, and it never sees your fit.

Never sent

  • Other page words, and anything you type
  • The page address or title
  • Your fit, or who you are

Worth knowing

The outline’s shape can show which site it came from.

What is kept

WebAble’s server keeps a count for each account, to limit how often this runs, and deletes it after 2 days. It never keeps the outline. OpenAI doesn’t use it for training and may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse. This device keeps the plan for 14 days.

Product learning

Off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the extension reports whether changes helped, as coarse counts with random noise added on your device. Your account is used to check you turned this on and to allow one report every 4 weeks. It isn’t stored with the counts. Totals are only published for groups of 50 devices or more, so no one person’s answers can be picked out.

Download or delete

  • Download your data from Privacy in your account: one file with everything your account holds.
  • Delete your account from the same page. It removes your account and everything tied to it, all at once.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes what it kept in that browser.

WebAble uses the data it handles only to fit pages to you. It is never used for ads, sold, or used to judge anyone. Its use of information from Chrome follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (opens in a new tab), including the Limited Use requirements.
